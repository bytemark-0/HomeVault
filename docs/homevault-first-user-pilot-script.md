# HomeVault First-User Pilot Script

**Prepared:** June 23, 2026  
**Purpose:** Give the beta operator a repeatable, non-leading script for the five-user P0 onboarding pilot.

---

## Session Goal

Observe whether a first-time user can understand HomeVault, create a home, add a useful record, and reach the dashboard without coaching.

---

## Moderator Rules

- Do not explain what HomeVault does before the session starts.
- Do not point to controls unless the participant is fully stuck and the session has already produced the finding.
- Ask neutral follow-up questions like "What are you looking for?" or "What do you expect to happen next?"
- Record hesitation, backtracking, and confusion even if the participant eventually succeeds.
- Do not collect private addresses, serial numbers, or document contents in notes.

---

## Session Setup

- Install a clean preview build.
- Start from a fresh install with no existing data.
- Have one sample photo and one sample PDF available if the participant wants to use them.
- Prepare a timer.
- Open the observation sheet before the participant starts.

---

## Moderator Intro

Read this verbatim:

> Thanks for helping test HomeVault. Please think out loud as you go. I did not build this specifically for you, so you will not hurt my feelings. I am interested in what is confusing or easy. I may ask what you are thinking, but I will not coach you unless you get completely blocked.

---

## Tasks

Ask the participant to:

1. Open the app and tell me what you think it does.
2. Set up your home.
3. Add a property photo, or skip it if you prefer.
4. Add one useful record that you would actually want to keep.
5. Find the main dashboard and tell me what you would do next.
6. If you notice a sample/demo option, feel free to explore it.
7. Close the app and reopen it.
8. Find where you would go to change your home photo or ask for help.

---

## What To Record

For each participant, record:

- Whether they understood the product in the first 10 seconds
- Time to create the first home
- Time to first useful record
- Whether they completed the flow without assistance
- Whether they confused sample data with real data
- Where they hesitated, backtracked, or got stuck
- Whether they noticed privacy/support information
- Whether they understood what the dashboard was telling them

---

## Severity Guide

- `P0`: crash, data loss, restore failure, onboarding dead end, sample data mistaken for real data
- `P1`: strong confusion, major layout break, repeated failure without workaround
- `P2`: polish, copy, hierarchy, or friction issue with a clear workaround

## Exit Criteria

- At least 4 of 5 participants explain the app correctly.
- At least 4 of 5 create a home and first useful record.
- No participant believes sample data is their real data.
- Any P0 finding is fixed before widening the beta.
